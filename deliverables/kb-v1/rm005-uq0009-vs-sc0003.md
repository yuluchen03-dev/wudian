### 2.5 The exact divergence

The two queries differ at **precisely one stage** — and it is *not* the stage that fails:

> **`SYNONYM_MAP` (`rag_service.py:208-261`) has no entry for 离世 / 亲人 / 死亡 / 去世 / 哀悼.**
> `_expand_synonyms(uq-0009) == uq-0009` (identity expansion), while
> `_expand_synonyms(sc-0003) == "如何面对失败 败 失"` (expands).
>
> **Identity expansion is fatal, because of `rag_service.py:538`:**
> ```python
> query_terms = self._extract_keywords(query) + self._extract_keywords(expanded_query)
> ```
> When `expanded_query == query`, the term list is **concatenated with itself**, so *every* term scores
> **twice**. Measured: uq-0009 → 14 + 14 = 28 terms (both halves `identical=True`); sc-0003 → 10 + 10 = 20.
> The `score / 8.0` normalisation at `rag_service.py:567` was calibrated for single counting, so
> identity-expanded queries receive a **~2× score inflation**, and the effective `DEBATE_MIN_SIMILARITY`
> floor of 0.10 is really ~0.05 for them.

uq-0009 has **no topical synonym to expand into**, so it collapses onto the un-expanded character-level surface —
and that surface (`人`, `世`, `对`) is the most frequent surface in the corpus.

**uq-0009's 9 hits are 100% produced by single-character terms**; zero survive on multi-character evidence.
Corroborating negative control: **`请问地铁几点停运` — pure subway-schedule logistics with no philosophical
content — returns 4 hits** via the same mechanism. The keyword path carries **no support-discrimination signal
at all** for un-expanded queries.

### Why single characters fire

`_extract_keywords` (`rag_service.py:1491-1524`) emits, for every 2+ char segment, the segment *and* its bigrams
*and* its individual characters. For uq-0009 those singles are `何 面 对 亲 人 离 世`, of which:
- `人` is scored against the **translation** field, which every verse carries;
- the match target `item["text"]` is the **concatenation of book + chapter + original + translation + keywords**
  (`rag_service.py:452-457`).

So an unrelated verse whose translation merely contains 人 (人 / 人生 / 别人 / 人与人) scores positive, is appended at
`rag_service.py:567`, clears the 0.10 floor at 0.25, and is counted by the Gate-E harness as 「本派原典证据」.

## 4. Primary root cause — exactly one

### **C — weak evidence binder** (primary)

The binder converting *retrieved items* into *admissible evidence* has **no relevance gate**. It admits any item
with `score > 0`, which a single common character achieves. The refusal branch then reads the **count** of admitted
items, so "we retrieved something" is silently treated as "we have evidence for this claim".

**Concrete code reference** — `backend/app/services/rag_service.py:526-570`:

```python
538:        query_terms = self._extract_keywords(query) + self._extract_keywords(expanded_query)
...
550:            for term in query_terms:
551:                if term in original:
552:                    score += 1.5
553:                elif term in translation or term in text:
554:                    score += 1.0          # <-- bare `人` scores 1.0, doubled by line 538
...
564:            if score <= 0:            # <-- the ONLY admissibility gate: no relevance floor
565:                continue
567:            scored.append({**item, "similarity": min(score / 8.0, 1.0)})
```

and at the consumption site — `eval/scripts/run_eval.py:250-253`:

```python
250:        hits = 0
251:        for sage in ("confucius", "laozi", "mozi", "hanfeizi"):
252:            res = asyncio.run(rag.retrieve_for_debate_sage(sage, query, top_k=3))
253:            hits += len(res.get("verses", []))     # <-- length, not relevance
```

**Reproducible probe**: `deliverables/kb-v1/dsh-rm005-negctl.py`, final section — removing only single-character
terms moves uq-0009 from **9 hits → 0 hits** while leaving the genuinely-supported `我该不该躺平` at **5 hits**.
`dsh-rm005-micro.py` attributes each of the 9 hits to one doubled single character.

**Why not the alternatives** — each ruled out by measurement, not assertion:

| Candidate | Ruled out because |
|---|---|
| A irrelevant evidence treated sufficient | This *describes the symptom*; C names the mechanism (no admissibility gate in the binder). Kept as the observation. |

The trust layer never sees it; `citation_guard.py:271-285` even sets `similarity: None`. |
| D fallback quote | **Ruled out.** uq-0009 never reaches the `retrieve_for_sage_chat` fallback (keyword results are non-empty, so `rag_service.py:1278` is never entered). sc-0003's one fallback item is not the failure. |
| E evidence sufficiency contract hole | Real and adjacent, but it is the **harness-side** hole (`hits == 0` as the sufficiency test). A *contributing* factor, not uq-0009's cause: even a correct contract would still receive 9 purportedly-relevant items. |

## 5. Is uq-0009 genuinely unsupported? — **YES, confirmed independently**

`trust-verification` (t3) asked me to confirm this independently. **Confirmed, on corpus evidence.**

Audit of the complete keyword inventory in `data/processed/debate_classics.json`, across **all sages**:

- **90 distinct keywords total.**
- Scanning for death / grief / mourning / bereavement / burial / ancestor cognates —
  `死 亡 丧 哀 悲 痛 泣 殁 殒 逝 终 命 鬼 神 祭 葬 哭 泪 别 离 苦 老 病 亲 家 孝 孤 寡`:
  - **`痛` → `痛快`** (joy — a false friend)
  - **`命` → `非命`** (Mozi's "against fatalism" — a doctrine, not mortality)
  - **`家` → `家国`** (nation, not family bereavement)
  - **every other cognate: zero hits.**

There is **no death, grief, mourning, or bereavement concept anywhere in the indexed debate corpus.** uq-0009 is
therefore **genuinely, corpus-grounded unsupported** — not merely "harder to retrieve". The refusal it should
receive is the *correct* answer; the system is wrong to answer it. This confirms Gate E is measuring a **real**
product failure, not a fixture artifact.

Note the consequence: the failure is **not** that uq-0009 needs better *retrieval* — the corpus has nothing to
retrieve. It needs a **relevance/sufficiency gate** able to say "these hits share only a common character".

## 6. Secondary contributing factors (separate from the primary cause)

1. **Harness sufficiency contract hole (E).** `eval/scripts/run_eval.py:254` defines sufficiency solely as
   `hits == 0`. Any single spurious hit makes refusal structurally unreachable. This is why C manifests as a *gate*
   failure rather than merely a degraded answer. **Must not be fixed by editing the frozen harness.**

2. **Synonym-map coverage gap at the query spine.** `SYNONYM_MAP` contains `失败`, `悲伤`, `生死`, `命运`, `鬼神` —
   but **no** `离世`, `亲人`, `去世`, `死亡`, `哀悼`, `丧亲`. uq-0009 is the query shape the map was not designed
   for, and identity expansion then *doubles* its score via the `:538` concatenation. Notably `生死` and `鬼神`
   entries already exist and do nothing here.

3. **Double-counting of the term list.** `rag_service.py:538` duplicates the full list whenever `_expand_synonyms`
   is a no-op (measured 14+14 and 10+10, both halves `identical=True`). Combined with the `/8.0` constant chosen for
   single counting, identity-expanded queries receive ~2× inflation.

4. **Single-character terms carry equal weight to meaningful bigrams.** `rag_service.py:1518-1523` deliberately
   emits single Chinese characters ("用于古文匹配，如 学 仁 礼") and `:550-554` scores them against the *translation*
   field. This is the direct supply of false positives — and it is a **deliberate design choice made for Recall@5**
   (FIX-003). Tightening it trades against Gate F; that tension must be **decided, not patched silently**.

5. **`similarity` is presented to users as a percentage without calibration.** Independently verified:
   `libai-jiangjinjiu-01` (「人生得意须尽欢，莫使金樽空对月。」) scores **6.0 / sim 0.7500** for uq-0009 — driven
   *entirely* by `对` + `人` each matched twice in the original. A character coincidence is rendered to the user as
   **75% relevance**. This is a **trust-signal defect in its own right**, independent of Gate E, user-visible via
   `build_debate_context` (`rag_service.py:1450`).

6. **Gate E iterates only 4 of the 9 indexed sages.** The index also holds `li_bai`, `su_shi`, `fan_zhongyan`,
   `wang_anshi`, `zhuge_liang` (independently verified). `eval_unsupported_refusal` never queries them, so
   `libai-jiangjinjiu-01` — the highest-scoring spurious hit in the system — is invisible to Gate E, while the same
   defect *does* reach `li_bai` (sc-0013) and `fan_zhongyan` (sc-0015) inside Gate J's 19-item denominator. The
   measurement **under-reports** the defect's true blast radius.

*Factors 5 and 6 and the 9-sage roster were first surfaced by `trust-verification` (t3). I re-derived each
independently in `dsh-rm005-tvcheck.py` and reproduce their numbers exactly (libai 6.0/0.7500 for uq-0009,
3.0/0.3750 for sc-0003; term duplication 28 and 20, `identical=True`).*

## 7. Constraint the fix must respect

`docs/kb-v1/DSH-RM005-SPEC.md` requirement 1 ("uq-0009 must produce zero hits") and the standing captain ruling
that **G5 is forbidden** are in direct tension:

- Zeroing uq-0009 by suppressing single-character / translation-field matches **also zeroes sc-0003's** keyword hits
  (`mozi-feigong-01` at 2.0 via `对`; `hanfeizi-wai-chu-shuo-01` at 3.0 via `败`).
- Independently measured: sc-0003 would keep **only** the confucius `2.20` fallback (sim 0.1125) and drop from
  **3 hits → 1 hit**.

This agrees with the standing ruling ("G5 收紧会清掉 sc-0003 合法命中"), so the constraint is **not new** — but the
reason is now established concretely rather than assumed: **sc-0003 survives on the identical mechanism as
uq-0009.** The two queries are **not distinguishable by any threshold on this signal.** A fix that separates them
must introduce evidence *relevance* semantics (concept/claim-level binding), not a tighter character filter.
Reported as a factual constraint; disposition belongs to the captain.
